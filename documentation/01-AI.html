01 · How I used AI

AI for speed and accuracy.
All final design decisions are mine.

I guided Claude like an intern: gathering and organizing research, keeping the sources straight, and turning my wireframes into components and a working prototype. All output was scrutinized and corrected by me.

What Claude did
  • Gathered and organized the research on 13 products
  • Executed this presentation. Acted as a sounding board
  • Built one exploration mock with all features from competitor analysis on it, to see trade-offs side by side
  • Turned my Figma wireframe direction into components and a working prototype
  • Drafted text from my dictated outline and decisions
What I decided
  • The product name, the users and all assumptions
  • The research direction and insights derived
  • The sketches, the wireframes and the information architecture
  • Every design call in the screens and the prototype
  • Directed and edited all text
