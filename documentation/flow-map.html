Alerts flow map

One averaged alerts experience, drawn as a flowchart. Every diamond is a decision the analyst makes and splits two ways. The chart merges the flows of the products in the research report; it is not any one product.

Screen the analyst sees Decision, two ways out Action Recorded, back to the queue Parked with an owner

The 13 decisions, in order

  1. Is the queue small enough to work?
  2. Can I decide from the row alone?
  3. Is there enough here to judge it?
  4. Is the alert real?
  5. Is only part of it wrong?
  6. Should future alerts like this change?
  7. Can I handle it now?
  8. Did the AI already act?
  9. Keep each automated action?
  10. Can the response be undone?
  11. Does the end user need to be involved?
  12. Did they answer in time?
  13. Does it need to go outside security?

Where each piece comes from

  • Queue counts, narrowing and AI buckets: Netskope, Matters.AI, Purview.
  • Deciding from the row: Matters.AI verdict column, Netskope status dropdown.
  • Investigate options: Matters.AI evidence sections, Varonis assistant and events, Cyberhaven history.
  • Partial false matches and teaching the AI: Purview, Nightfall.
  • Close reasons: Varonis.
  • AI already acted: Matters.AI automated remediation.
  • End user justification and expiry: Nightfall, Cyberhaven.
  • Hand off outside security: Cyberhaven.
  • The "can it be undone" split and the timed-out branch are my additions from the Ideate page, not observed in a product.
