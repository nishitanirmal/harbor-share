06 · The AI workflow

What the AI decides, what the analyst decides

Harbor acts alone only where the action can be undone. Anything irreversible waits for a person. And every correction is logged.

Decides, each with an undo
  • The severity
  • The one-line summary
  • Auto-closing low-risk alerts, listed under Auto-closed with Reopen case
  • Reversible fixes, such as revoking a share link, with Restore link
Only suggests
  • The verdict, in Harbor's call
  • Recommended next steps
  • Grouping alerts into an incident, under Harbor suggests
  • Irreversible actions, which wait for a named confirmation
Prioritization logic

Alerts are listed by severity, with alerts on high-value assets first. Each alert carries a Priority and Reasoning section that says what moved it.

WeighsSensitivity of the data
WeighsExposure: internal, external, public
WeighsDistance from the user's and device's baseline
WeighsWhether the asset is marked critical
Listed firstAlerts on high-value assets sit under High-value assets · Critical, above Other alerts
How its reasoning is shown
  • A short Summary in plain words
  • Data lineage shows each hop with an icon, and a risky step says so in words, such as "Unusual destination for this user"
  • Harbor's call says what it did and why, and the details page adds Priority and Reasoning
  • While a query runs, Harbor says so and the list shows row skeletons
Confirming, restoring and marking as expected

Harbor's call carries three buttons.

ConfirmRestore linkMark as expected
  • Confirm keeps what Harbor did and sets the verdict to True positive.
  • Restore link brings the link back, then asks whether to edit the response or change the verdict.
  • Mark as expected sets the verdict to Expected activity and opens Teach Harbor.
What happens to a correction
1Logged on the alert with your name and time2Shows in the Audit trail3Teach Harbor asks why, with reasons to pick or your own4False positive and Expected activity stay separate verdicts

None of the products studied showed what happened to a rejection afterwards. In the prototype the reason is logged. How Harbor learns from it is not built.

What is reversible
Reversible

Harbor does it at once and the alert says what it did. Restore link undoes it.

Not reversible

A flag icon and the words "This action is irreversible", with a button that names the object. Only these actions get one.
