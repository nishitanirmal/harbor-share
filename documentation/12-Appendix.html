Full AI method, research documents, sources

Everything claimed in How I used AI, written up step by step, with the documents behind the research.

Full AI method
Research

Claude gathered public documentation and screenshots for 13 products, wrote them up to a fixed template, and built the flowcharts and the comparison matrix. I set the products, the criteria and the judgments, and removed anything that could not be traced to a source.

Feature inventory

Claude compiled every CTA, information category and chart type across the products into one Feature Definition, by tool, with merged groups.

Design exploration

Claude Design produced one alerts page carrying every feature from the inventory, plus one variant for each place where the products made opposite choices. Used to see trade-offs side by side before sketching. Part of the ideation, not a final screen.

Wireframes and UI

Mine, in Procreate and Figma.

Components and prototype

Claude Code turned my Figma screens into components and a working prototype. I reviewed every screen against the wireframes and corrected it.

Writing

Claude drafted structure and text from my dictated outline and decisions; I edited every section. The product carries the working name Harbor so nothing here claims to show the Matters.AI product.
